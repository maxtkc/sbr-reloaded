Graduate students at St John’s College typically live in the College’s hostels, located around Cambridge. These include older, more affordable terraced houses and larger, newer homes, which are more spacious but farther from the town center. Traditional college-style accommodation is also available.

Rooms vary in size and rent, with most containing a sink and basic furniture like a bed, desk, wardrobe, and shelves. Many rooms have wireless internet, connected to the College’s network. Bathrooms, showers, and kitchens are shared, with kitchens offering communal spaces and storage but no provided cooking equipment. Most hostels have common rooms and are equipped with some appliances, such as microwaves and kettles, left behind by past residents.
