The Graduate Study Room or GSR is located on the second floor of **1 All Saints Passage**, behind the Old Divinity School, and can be accessed with your Cam Card. This study space is exclusively for postgraduates. There are monitors, desk space, whiteboards, and other amenities. There is also a reading alcove with some couches for relaxing in between study sessions.

For detailed directions on how to access the room, please click [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ViIfPf_Ost24ANLH86J42SI4dee2Lo6j/view?usp=sharing).
