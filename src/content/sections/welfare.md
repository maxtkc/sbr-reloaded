Starting a new part of your life can be exciting, but it can also be stressful and intimidating. Finding the right person to talk to can make life much, much easier.

**Contacts in college.**

- Your **College tutor** is available for non-academic matters, including financial support, personal difficulties, problems with your supervisor, etc.

- The **College Nurses** are trained and confidential counsellors as well. Please get in touch with the Nurses if you would like to be referred to the college counsellor.

- **The Chaplain**, is available as a confidential listener for all members of college, whatever their religious convictions.

- **Student Services** (F staircase Chapel Court) can help with College issues, such as bills, student cards etc.

- All new students are given a **graduate buddy** who can help you settle into college and answer any questions you may have. They’ll meet up with you at least twice during the first term, and be a friendly face as you get used to Cambridge postgraduate life.

- The [**SBR Committee**](/#committee)welcomes questions, and **welfare officers** are especially available to direct you to the appropriate people/places.

**Welfare Resource Pack.** For a comprehensive list of resources that are available you within college, the university, and the city of Cambridge, see the [Welfare Resource Pack](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1INMCPM8OPaQKwzOlnjgWoykwurdkWop9/view).

**Anonymous welfare request form.** Your welfare officers are committed to helping you with any questions or concerns you may have. We acknowledge that sometimes it can be challenging to come forward with questions or concerns. This is why we have created an [anonymous form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfjY5agNiow7gJexh4cxgAel1h7_TynLoeyh7Z_o28Cp60mWA/viewform) with space for you to ask a question or make a welfare-related request.It will be forwarded to the welfare officer(s). All responses are completely anonymous, and no email addresses or CRS IDs will be traceable.
