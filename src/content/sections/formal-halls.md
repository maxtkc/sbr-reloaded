- **Postgrads dine with fellows.** Every term, fellows invite postgraduate students to dine with them at high table. Tickets are free, but limited. They are available on [UPay](https://www.upay.co.uk/app/). Postgrads should not attend more than once per academic year.

- **BA Table.** Every Tuesday and Friday during term, one of the three hall tables is reserved for graduate students. Be sure to purchase your BA Table ticket in advance on [UPay](https://www.upay.co.uk/app/), as it often sells out. Tickets are exclusive to graduates.

- **Swaps.** Swaps are inter-collegiate dining events where we dine at other Cambridge colleges and invite them to dine with us. These events may include parties, and we also have swaps with colleges like Balliol (Oxford), Trinity (Dublin), Ghislieri (Italy), and Uppsala (Sweden).

- **Port & Sherry.** Port & Sherry is offered in the SBR after hall on Fridays, for just £1.50 per glass.
