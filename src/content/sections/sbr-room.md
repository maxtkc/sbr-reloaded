The Samuel Butler Room (SBR), located in the **corner of First Court**, is the heart of the SBR society. It’s a space for social events, and serves port and sherry before and after BA Table. Accessible to all St John’s graduates with a University Card, the room offers a quiet, comfortable environment for studying, relaxing, or meeting peers. Inside, you'll find a TV with a Wii, board games, a pool table, a piano, and a small kitchen. We encourage graduates to use the space responsibly and tidy up after use so everyone can enjoy it.
