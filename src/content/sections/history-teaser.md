The Samuel Butler Room Society was founded in 1960 as a society for the postgraduate students of St John's College (what most other colleges call their MCR or Middle Combination Room). The society and postgraduate common room are named in honour of Samuel Butler, a Victorian author, polymath, and sheep farmer who was a student at St John's from 1854 to 1858. You can read more about the man himself [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Butler_%28novelist%29) and more about the founding of the SBR [here](/history).
